Delete Your Account

Variety H2O + Ice Cream app · Effective October 15, 2026

You can ask us to delete your Variety H2O + Ice Cream app account (on Google Play and at varietyh2o.com) at any time. This page explains how, and what happens to your data.

How to request deletion

  1. Email gkcompany2019@gmail.com from the email address on your account, with the subject "Delete my account". Include your name and, if you know it, your customer ID.
  2. Or ask our staff in person at the store.
  3. We may contact you to confirm your identity, then delete your account within 30 days and email you when it's done.

Before you delete: any prepaid gallon balance left on your account is lost when the account is deleted. Use your remaining gallons first, or ask our staff about it when you make your request.

What we delete

  • Your name, email address, and customer ID.
  • Your login, including your password and any Sign in with Google link.
  • Your preferred water type, membership date, and gallon balance.

Settings stored only on your device (such as biometric unlock) are removed when you uninstall the App or clear its data.
