Privacy Policy

Variety H2O + Ice Cream app · Effective September 26, 2026

This policy explains what information the Variety H2O + Ice Cream app (the "App"), available on Google Play and at varietyh2o.com, collects, how we use it, and who it is shared with. "We" means Variety H2O + Ice Cream, Tucson, Arizona.

1. Information we collect

Account information. Your name, email address, and a customer ID. Your account is created by our staff or by you using a one-time claim code we give you in person. If you sign in with email and password, your password is stored only in hashed form by our authentication provider; we cannot read it.

Prepaid water account data. Your current gallon balance, your transaction history (credits and debits with dates and any note our staff enter), your membership date, and your preferred water type.

Sign in with Google (optional). If you choose to sign in with Google, Google shares your Google account email address and basic profile information with us. We use your email address to match your existing customer account.

Session and device data. Your device stores a sign-in session token so you stay signed in. If you turn on Face ID, Touch ID, or fingerprint unlock, your device verifies you locally; we never receive, see, or store your biometric data. The App saves only a setting on your device recording that unlock is turned on.

Server logs. Our hosting provider automatically records standard technical information such as your IP address, browser or device type, and the time of each request.

We do not collect your precise or approximate location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data, or payment card information. The App does not use advertising or analytics tools.

2. How we use information

  • To create and maintain your account and show your gallon balance and history.
  • To let our staff record credits and debits to your account at our store.
  • To sign you in and keep your account secure.
  • To keep the App working, prevent misuse, and fix problems.

We do not sell your personal information and do not use it for advertising.

The App links to outside services (Facebook, online ordering, Google Maps, Apple Maps, and phone calling). Those services have their own privacy policies, and we do not control them.

4. Retention

We keep your account and transaction history for as long as your account is active. When you ask us to delete your account, we delete or anonymize your personal information, except where we must keep certain records to meet legal or accounting obligations.

5. Security

Information travels between the App and our servers over encrypted (HTTPS) connections, and access to customer records is restricted by account permissions so customers can see only their own data. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your choices and rights

  • You can request a copy of the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete your account.
  • You can turn biometric unlock on or off in the App's Settings at any time, and sign out at any time.
  • You can stop using Sign in with Google and use your email and password instead.

To make a request, email us at gkcompany2019@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity before acting on it.

7. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top shows when it was last changed, and material changes will be reflected here.
